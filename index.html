02 — Selected work

Five projects, opened up.

Problem, the decision I made, and what changed. No vanity metrics — where a number could not be trusted, it isn't here.

Paid media

Multi-platform paid campaigns

Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn against an annual budget above MYR 2M.

Problem

Four platforms, four auctions, different attribution windows — and no single view of which channel produced a qualified enquiry rather than a cheap click.

Decision

Kept a fixed weekly review cadence judged on cost per qualified enquiry, moved budget toward channels producing enquiries that reached a human, and cut search-term waste with negative keyword hygiene. I owned allocation; creative sat with design.

Result

Consistent enquiry flow across the year and a channel mix defended with data instead of preference.

Google Ads · Meta Ads · TikTok Ads · LinkedIn Ads · GA4

Analytics

Tracking and reporting implementation

GA4 and Google Tag Manager rebuilt across group websites and campaign pages.

Problem

Conversions, behaviour, and attribution were guesswork. Reports could not answer which campaign produced an enquiry.

Decision

Defined the events that actually mattered first — enquiry, form start, form submit, WhatsApp click — implemented them in GTM, then aligned GA4 conversions and Looker Studio reporting to the same definitions.

Result

One shared set of definitions between marketing and stakeholders, and campaign decisions traceable back to a source.

GA4 · Google Tag Manager · Looker Studio · Search Console

Web platform

Group website management

12+ WordPress sites on one consistent, secure, maintainable footing.

Problem

Inconsistent structure across a dozen sites made publishing slow and maintenance expensive.

Decision

Standardised structured content using Custom Post Types and Advanced Custom Fields, hardened performance and security, and fixed technical SEO foundations site by site.

Result

Faster publishing, more stable sites, better mobile usability, and a content model non-technical colleagues could actually use.

WordPress · ACF · Custom Post Types · Cloudflare · Search Console

CRM & automation

SleekFlow WhatsApp chatbot journey

Enquiry qualification automated — recognised with SleekFlow's Automation Architect Award.

Problem

Enquiry volume outran manual response, and slow first replies were losing qualified leads.

Decision

Designed the qualification flow around the questions that actually determined lead quality, captured them at the start, then routed conversations to the right follow-up owner.

Result

Faster, consistent journey from enquiry to follow-up — awarded best-performing partner implementation by the vendor.

SleekFlow · WhatsApp · CRM automation · lead routing

Technical SEO

Technical SEO and site performance

Crawlability, Core Web Vitals, and index health, sorted by traffic impact.

Problem

Slow templates and indexing issues were capping organic visibility across the group's sites.

Decision

Audited structure, page speed, and indexing with Screaming Frog and Search Console, then prioritised by traffic impact rather than by ease of fix — several "quick wins" were rejected because the pages carried almost no traffic.

Result

Faster pages, a cleaner index, and stronger foundations for search performance.

Screaming Frog · Search Console · Ahrefs · SEMrush · Core Web Vitals
