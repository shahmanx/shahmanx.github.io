Case study 01 · Higher education acquisition

Paid media for student acquisition

Role
Campaign planning, execution and optimisation
Platforms
Google Ads · Meta · TikTok · LinkedIn
Scope
RM2M+ annual budget across programmes and intakes

Context

Institution and programme-level recruitment needs campaigns that run continuously, then tighten around intake deadlines. Intent ranges from broad awareness to specific programme searches, and each programme has its own audience and enquiry profile.

What I did

Set up and ran campaigns across four platforms: keyword and audience structure per programme, ad and creative coordination with the design team, spend allocation and phasing by intake cycle, and weekly monitoring against enquiry quality rather than click cost alone.

Optimisation decisions

Moved budget toward channels and ad groups producing enquiries that reached a human, cut search terms that produced volume without enquiries, and restructured landing page entry points when the highest-traffic programme pages weren't converting.

What I owned

Campaign structure, budget allocation, optimisation, reporting. Creative production and content sat with the design and content teams.

Case study 02 · Marketing technology and attribution

Salesforce Web-to-Lead and campaign attribution

Role
Hands-on implementation (completed) and requirements owner (in progress)
Stack
Salesforce Web-to-Lead · hidden UTM fields · landing pages
Status
Web-to-Lead live · embedded iframe form work ongoing with in-house team

Context

Campaign leads were arriving through landing page forms, but the source of each lead had to be reconstructed after the fact. Without the campaign origin stored on the lead record, reporting could describe volume but not which campaign produced it.

What I implemented myself

Configured the Salesforce Web-to-Lead HTML forms on campaign landing pages, added hidden UTM fields so campaign source travelled with the submission, and connected landing page form submissions to Salesforce lead capture.

Why it matters

Lead source is now captured at the point of submission rather than inferred later. That is what makes campaign-level reporting possible and lets recruitment follow-up see where an enquiry originated.

Conceptual flow of the live Web-to-Lead setup. Technical details of the embedded form work are still being defined with the in-house development team and are not published here.

Case study 03 · Measurement

GA4, GTM and campaign measurement

Role
Hands-on implementation and reporting
Stack
GA4 · Google Tag Manager · Looker Studio · Search Console
Scope
Group websites, campaign landing pages, enquiry events

Context

Across a group of institution sites, conversion and behaviour data existed in fragments. Reporting could show traffic, but not reliably which pages and campaigns produced enquiries.

What I did

Defined the events that mattered first — enquiry submissions, form starts, and WhatsApp clicks — implemented them in Google Tag Manager, then aligned GA4 conversions and Looker Studio reporting to those same definitions so marketing and stakeholders were reading the same numbers.

Boundaries of the data

GA4 attribution describes on-site behaviour and campaign-driven conversions. It is not the same as Salesforce lead-source reporting, and it does not track downstream enrolment outcomes. Where measurement could not be trusted, I report it as unmeasured rather than approximate it.

Case study 04 · Lead operations

SleekFlow WhatsApp lead qualification

Role
Flow design and rollout
Stack
SleekFlow · WhatsApp · CRM lead routing
Scope
Enquiry qualification and follow-up routing

Context

Enquiry volume outran manual first response, and slow replies were costing qualified enquiries. The team needed consistent qualification without adding headcount.

What I did

Designed the WhatsApp qualification flow around the questions that actually determined whether an enquiry was viable, captured those details up front, then routed conversations to the right follow-up owner so handoffs were explicit rather than implicit.

Cross-team work

Worked with the teams handling follow-up to agree what "qualified" meant in practice, so the flow reflected the follow-up process rather than an idealised version of it.

Supporting work

100+ landing pages and 12+ websites

Built and maintained conversion-oriented landing pages alongside a group-wide WordPress estate: structured content, template consistency, and the page-level changes that follow from campaign and analytics findings. WordPress work here covers content structure and delivery, not custom application development.

Technical SEO and site performance

Ran technical audits and fixed what mattered first: crawlability, indexing, page speed and Core Web Vitals. Priorities were set by traffic impact rather than by how easy each fix was, which meant deliberately skipping some "quick wins" on pages carrying almost no traffic.

How I work

The acquisition chain as I actually work it, stage by stage, with what I do at each point.

  1. Media strategy

    Translate intake and programme objectives into channel mix, audience structure, budget phasing and the KPIs the reporting will be judged against.

  2. Campaign execution

    Build and run campaigns across Google, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn, coordinating creative and copy with the design and content teams.

  3. Landing page

    Build and adjust programme-specific landing pages so the message matches the ad and the form asks only for what follow-up actually needs.

  4. Conversion tracking

    Define enquiry events in GTM and GA4 first, then verify they fire correctly — because an untracked conversion makes every optimisation decision a guess.

  5. CRM integration

    Carry campaign source to the lead record through Web-to-Lead forms and hidden UTM fields, so attribution is stored at submission rather than reconstructed later.

  6. Lead quality

    Read platform numbers next to what the follow-up teams actually received. Volume without qualification is not a result.

  7. Optimisation

    Move budget, structure and pages on what the data shows, then document the decision so the next cycle starts from evidence rather than memory.

Enrolment and revenue outcomes sit with the institution's recruitment and admissions process. I work on acquisition and measurement up to the point of qualified enquiry.
